Sphere Authority

body
Src Body Matrix mode solves Y from gravity/buoyancy/drag/wave slope. Cursor/auto target movement applies force through mass, radius, air/water viscosity, drag, and added mass; there is no artificial max-speed cap.

Src Sphere Body Coupling

Planing, skim, and slam are derived from speed, mass, wetted area, density ratio, Froude, entry angle, contact age, and sampled wave slope. They are not direct knobs.

aqua
SDF Contact uses the sphere equation to vary the water/object boundary with height, avoiding the cylinder-footprint artifact. Sheet Shell is an experimental pseudo-wrap visualization.

Pass18 Visual Authority

Render-only transplant controls. The solver still owns eta/vx/vz/sigma/FSS/JIT; these controls only interpret pressure, risk, particles, and height as optics/aeration.

Src Pool / Caustic Domain

v2
Src-style adjustable pool domain. Size/depth now drive mesh scale, wall collision, pool receiver, shader ray-box tests, DX/CFL, and solid-aware flux reflection.

Parametric Pool Tile Construction

stack
First construction pass: real raised tile plates over recessed grout substrates on floor, walls, and coping strips. Layout is solved in world units with symmetric cuts and a geometry cap for very large pools.

Folded Surface Sheet — Phase 2

Phase 2 treats the top surface as a mass-carrying sheet with inertia: thickness/director/fold/proximity pressure are state fields, not render-only decoration.

Release Collapse — Phase 3

Phase 3 converts negative sigmaDot into cavity collapse: the sphere opening new capacity makes the sheet detach, fall inward, and rebound through SWE instead of sticking to the sphere skin.

Sheet Failure Export — Phase 4

Phase 4 computes failed-sheet volume, ejection energy, and candidate area: the exact cells that can become JIT sheet-MPM in Phase 5.

JIT Sheet-MPM Spawn — Phase 5

Phase 5 emits temporary separated water only from failed-sheet reservoirs. These particles are not decorative spray: they carry exported sheet volume/velocity and re-couple into the SWE field on re-entry.

HF Energy Elongation — Bubble/Ribbon Gate

energy
This is the JIT-MPM precursor layer: it does not stamp the sphere footprint. It watches contact/momentum/pressure energy, stores an oriented elongation field, and turns failed sheet mass into ribbon/bubble-blower style export candidates.

Object Runup Film — Phase 6

off
Phase 6 treats the sphere as a runup object: receding water deposits a wet attached film, the film drains down the SDF surface, edges evaporate/shrink, and the weighted lower film emits droplets back into the sheet-MPM path.
