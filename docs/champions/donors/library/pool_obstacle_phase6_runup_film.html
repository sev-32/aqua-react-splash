SDF Contact uses the sphere equation to vary the water/object boundary with height, avoiding the cylinder-footprint artifact. Sheet Shell is an experimental pseudo-wrap visualization.

Folded Surface Sheet — Phase 2

Phase 2 treats the top surface as a mass-carrying sheet with inertia: thickness/director/fold/proximity pressure are state fields, not render-only decoration.

Release Collapse — Phase 3

Phase 3 converts negative sigmaDot into cavity collapse: the sphere opening new capacity makes the sheet detach, fall inward, and rebound through SWE instead of sticking to the sphere skin.

Sheet Failure Export — Phase 4

Phase 4 computes failed-sheet volume, ejection energy, and candidate area: the exact cells that can become JIT sheet-MPM in Phase 5.

JIT Sheet-MPM Spawn — Phase 5

Phase 5 emits temporary separated water only from failed-sheet reservoirs. These particles are not decorative spray: they carry exported sheet volume/velocity and re-couple into the SWE field on re-entry.

Object Runup Film — Phase 6

Phase 6 treats the sphere as a runup object: receding water deposits a wet attached film, the film drains down the SDF surface, edges evaporate/shrink, and the weighted lower film emits droplets back into the sheet-MPM path.
