Sphere Authority

body
Body mode solves vertical height from gravity, buoyancy, drag, added mass, planing, slam, wave slope, and pool-wall losses. There is no user Y-height slider; vertical position is physical except canonical scripted tests.

Advanced Sphere Body Coupling

Planing, skim, and slam are derived from sampled waterline, wetted area, speed, density ratio, Froude number, and entry angle. These are not sphere-footprint force stamps.

Solver Constants

Continuous swept solid sampling distributes fast sphere motion over the real path segment during each fluid substep, so quick drags do not produce checker/lumpy frequency wakes from frame-rate sampling.

SDF Contact uses the sphere equation to vary the water/object boundary with height, avoiding the cylinder-footprint artifact. Sheet Shell is an experimental pseudo-wrap visualization.

Pass18 Visual Authority

Beauty-lighting transplant layer: sun direction, fresnel/specular, absorption, environment reflection, and pool depth/AO are render-only controls. Caustics are generated from the Pass18 area-ratio refracted-light texture path, not a procedural sine lace.

Foam System

mixed
Foam is now a persistent state field. It is born from cresting, contact/impact, pressure, elongation, sheet failure, and JIT-MPM particle density, then decays independently instead of being only a one-frame shader mask.

Projected Material Boundary — Real HF Collision

Hard authority shift: water predicts first, then an SDF collision projection converts invalid object intersections into strain, pressure, reflected momentum, backside void, and refill flow. No sphere-shaped force stamp.

HF Energy Elongation — Bubble/Ribbon Gate

strain
Render/MPM staging field for bubble-blower ribbon behavior: persistent elongation is generated from selected HF energy/strain/void layers, not sphere position.

Folded Surface Sheet — Phase 2

Phase 2 treats the top surface as a mass-carrying sheet with inertia: thickness/director/fold/proximity pressure are state fields, not render-only decoration.

Release Collapse — Phase 3

Phase 3 converts negative sigmaDot into cavity collapse: the sphere opening new capacity makes the sheet detach, fall inward, and rebound through SWE instead of sticking to the sphere skin.

Sheet Failure Export — Phase 4

Phase 4 computes failed-sheet volume, ejection energy, and candidate area: the exact cells that can become JIT sheet export in Phase 5.

Disabled Separated-Fluid Export

Phase 5 emits temporary separated water from sheet stress reservoirs. V39 boosts this into a denser local particle-like fluid parcel layer: more parcels, smoother cohesive motion, surface-tension pullback, and multi-cell SWE recoupling on re-entry. These are still reduced particles, not a full pressure-projected FLIP solver.

Disabled Waterline Splash

Ports the uploaded React splash policy into this standalone branch: entry crown, exit sheet, submerged breach, continuous trailing foam particles, and settled-particle heightfield recoupling. It uses waterline/submergence gates, not raw sphere position.

Object Runup Film — Phase 6

off
Phase 6 treats the sphere as a runup object: receding water deposits a wet attached film, the film drains down the SDF surface, edges evaporate/shrink, and the weighted lower film emits droplets back into the sheet export path.
